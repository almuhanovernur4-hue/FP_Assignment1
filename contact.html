About us

We're community of people passionate with music that went under the radars for several reasons, and trying to share it with people that appreciate the experimental sound too

Most undeground artists start from posting on Soundcloud, which is a free platform for public audio posting

Once they have enough listeners and resources, they blow up into more mainstream fields, posting their music on Spotify, marking the true start of their career

They often collaborate with each other, creating tight but dense scene
